Privacy Policy — Beam Flash Light

Effective: 25 October 2026  ·  App: Beam Flash Light (package com.afzal.beam)  ·  Developer: Afzal E Services

Beam Flash Light is a flashlight app. It is designed to work entirely on your device, with no account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet connection.

Data we collect

None. Beam Flash Light does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. The app has no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere even if it wanted to.

Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy it is used
FlashlightTo switch the phone's LED torch on and off.
VibrateFor the optional haptic tick when you tap the power button.
Foreground service (special use)Only when you enable "Shake to toggle": to listen to the accelerometer so two quick shakes can switch the torch on or off.
NotificationsOnly to show the "flashlight is on" control and the shake-service notice, so you can switch the light off from the notification shade.

No permission is used to collect data. The camera is never accessed; the torch is controlled through the system's flashlight API only.

On-device storage

Your settings (such as start brightness, auto-off timer, and toggles) are stored only on your own device. They are never uploaded, backed up to any server, or shared with anyone.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services in the app. Nothing about you or your device is shared with any third party.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
